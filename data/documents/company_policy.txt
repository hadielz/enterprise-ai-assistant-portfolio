Travel Policy:
Employees can be reimbursed for train, flight, hotel, and meal expenses related to approved business trips.
Receipts are required for all expenses above 25 euros.
Hotel reimbursement is limited to 150 euros per night.
Meal reimbursement is limited to 30 euros per meal.

Remote Work Policy:
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.
Sensitive company data must not be stored on personal devices.

IT Support Policy:
Employees should create an IT ticket when they face login issues, software installation problems, or hardware failures.
Critical issues should be marked as urgent.
