HR Leave Policy:
Employees have 25 paid vacation days per year.
Vacation requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance.
Sick leave requires medical justification if it exceeds three consecutive days.
Parental leave follows local labor law and must be coordinated with HR.

Training Policy:
Employees may request up to 1,500 euros per year for professional training.
Training must be related to the employee's role or career development.
Manager approval is required before registration.
