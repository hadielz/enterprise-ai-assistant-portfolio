Finance Policy:
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days after the expense date.
Receipts are required for all expenses above 25 euros.
Meal expenses are reimbursed up to 30 euros per meal.
Hotel expenses are reimbursed up to 150 euros per night.
Taxi expenses require business justification.

Invoice Policy:
Supplier invoices must include a purchase order number.
Invoices above 5,000 euros require department head approval.
